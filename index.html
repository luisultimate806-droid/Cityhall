Popayán City Hall

Location & History

Popayán's Municipal Administrative Center (CAM) is located at Carrera 6 #4-21, in the Historic Center. It is an important public building within the city's protected heritage area.

Administrative Center

It is the main headquarters of the municipal government, where the Mayor's Office and several secretariats coordinate public programs, services, and projects for the city.

Nighttime Atmosphere

Its white façades and the nearby historic buildings create a distinctive atmosphere, especially when the streets and monuments are illuminated at night.

History of City Hall

The current Municipal Administrative Center (CAM) brings together buildings with a long history in Popayán's historic center.

17th century

A historical study by the Fundación Universitaria de Popayán, based on the book Muros de Bronce, explains that the original house was built by Captain Pedro de Velasco.

1707–1792

The same study records that the property was acquired by Governor Baltazar Carlos de Viveros in 1707 and later purchased by the Jesuits from the municipality in 1792.

1906

After the earthquake of 1906, the building was reconstructed with a neo-colonial appearance and some republican features, especially in its façade, windows, and balconies.

1983

Following the earthquake of 1983, the municipality acquired most of the properties in the block. Their interiors were connected, helping establish the Municipal Administrative Center known as the CAM.

Architecture & Heritage

The CAM is part of the historic urban landscape of Popayán. Its appearance combines traditional colonial references with later architectural details.

White Façade

The building has two levels, a white exterior, arched windows, balconies, and a central entrance. These features help it blend with the traditional architecture of the historic center.

Neo-colonial Character

A university study describes the CAM's architectural character as neo-colonial, with some republican elements. Its current appearance reflects different stages of reconstruction and adaptation.

Heritage Conservation

The same study identifies the City Hall building under a level of integral conservation. This means that interventions should respect its historic character and important architectural features.

Municipal Government & Services

The Mayor's Office coordinates local government through different secretariats and offices that work on the needs of the municipality.

Public Administration

The Mayor's Office directs the municipal administration and coordinates plans, programs, projects, and public resources for Popayán.

Municipal Secretariats

The administration includes areas such as Education, Government, Finance, Health, Infrastructure, Planning, and Transit, among others. Each one handles matters within its area of responsibility.

Citizen Services

Residents can request information, complete procedures, and submit petitions, complaints, or claims in person or through the municipality's online channels.
